SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 1 January 2025 between ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Client"), having its office at Chennai, and XYZ Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("Service Provider"), having its office at Coimbatore.

1. PURPOSE
The Service Provider shall design, develop and deliver a web-based inventory management software for the Client.

2. DURATION
This Agreement is effective from 1 January 2025 and shall remain in force for 12 months, ending on 31 December 2025, unless terminated earlier.

3. OBLIGATIONS
3.1 The Service Provider shall deliver the software in three milestones: 31 March 2025, 30 June 2025 and 30 September 2025.
3.2 The Client shall provide all required data, access and timely feedback.

4. PAYMENT TERMS
The Client shall pay a total fee of INR 12,00,000, payable in three equal instalments upon completion of each milestone. Invoices must be settled within 15 days of receipt. Late payments attract interest of 1.5% per month.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties shall keep all confidential information secret during the term and for 3 years after termination.

6. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days' written notice. Either party may terminate immediately if the other commits a material breach that is not cured within 15 days of notice.

7. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Disputes shall be referred to arbitration in Chennai.
